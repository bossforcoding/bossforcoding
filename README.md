# Hi, I'm Mattia Bosetti

Research Assistant at [SUPSI](https://www.supsi.ch), with a Bachelor's in Computer Engineering and a Master's in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence.

My work focuses on **AI for sustainability**, especially in the eco-design phase of products, where data-driven models, optimization algorithms and Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) help find more sustainable design choices early on. With a background as a full-stack developer, I also build **LLM-based multi-agent systems** and chatbots.
